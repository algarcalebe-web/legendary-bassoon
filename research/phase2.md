# Phase 2: what affluent travellers resort to, and where it fails (2026-10-06)

Scores are **our assessment (1-10)** of how well each alternative handles a pain *by design*. They are judgement, not measured outcomes. Evidence is mostly search-snippet level; detail and URLs in `raw/alt_a.md`, `raw/alt_b.md`, `raw/alt_c.md`. Pain numbers follow `phase1.md`.

Pain columns: **P1** research load · **P2** choice overload/regret · **P3** unreliable information · **P4** entry/border friction · **P5** planning stress and coordination · **P7** disruption/loss on trip · **P9** health/safety worry.

## Five failure patterns that cut across alternatives

| # | Pattern | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Risk stays with the traveller.** Fees, deposits and memberships are paid up front and no one guarantees the outcome. | 55% of advisors charge fees; deposits of US$100k-1M (Wheels Up, Signature) or US$175k+ (Exclusive Resorts); Jet It Chapter 7 (Dec 2025); Tanner & Haley members lost over US$200M (2006); Inspirato cancellation complaints are anecdotal | Medium |
| 2 | **Provider fragility.** The memberships and clubs built to remove worry keep failing or consolidating. | Quintessentially going-concern flag; John Paul down from 1,000 to ~400 staff; onefinestay retreating from three cities; Inspirato sold for ~US$59M equity; Wheels Up users -40% in Q1 2025 | Medium (snippet level) |
| 3 | **Demand for professional help exceeds use.** Travellers say advisors beat DIY, but few use them. | Harris/Preferred Hotels 2025 (n about 503, seen in summaries only): 84% say an advisor beats internet research, advisors used for 17% of past vacations, 53% intend to use one in 3 years. Flywire Jan 2026 (vendor): 92-97% of US luxury travellers rate advisors as best | Weak-medium. Strongest lead; verify against primaries |
| 4 | **Preferences are re-captured every time and the real brief is missed.** | Zicasso states forms cannot capture "the why"; each provider runs its own 15-45 minute call and form (Phase 1); 59% start with no destination (Expedia/Luth 2023). No data on repeated questions or generic plans | Weak. Hypothesis, not a finding |
| 5 | **Access, entry and safety are bought item by item, and nobody covers the whole trip.** P4 (borders) is handled by no alternative (scores 1-6). Premiums are large per item. | Vatican private access EUR 161-565 vs EUR 25 standard (9-23x); armoured Rio transfer from R$1,000 per trip, ~10x a taxi; resold restaurant tables US$2,100+; Colosseum private tours EUR 400 | Medium for prices; premiums prove willingness to pay at those points, not buyer volume |

## What this means for Phase 3 (hypotheses, not conclusions)
- No alternative handles P4 (border and entry friction) or P2 (regret), and the AI tools score low on trust (8% comfortable booking via AI).
- Revealed willingness to pay is clearest for access, security, transfers and rescue. It is weakest for planning time itself.
- Failures cluster around **who bears risk** and **who is still solvent**, not around the length of the interview.
