Carbon and traceability data packs for Brazilian exports to the EU: CBAM, EUDR and VSME, paid in EUR and priced on the money the buyer saves

October to December 2026 is a short window where demand is forced by deadlines. CBAM's definitive period started on 1 Jan 2026. Default values carry a 10% markup in 2026, 20% in 2027 and 30% from 2028. The first CBAM verifier accreditations are only arriving around Sep 2026, so Brazilian producers have no verified data yet. EUDR applies from 30 Dec 2026 for large and medium operators. The EU-Mercosur interim agreement has applied since 1 May 2026, and ApexBrasil counts about 761 SC firms already exporting to the EU. Brazilian charcoal-based pig iron and low-carbon production look cheap on paper but are penalised by default values (estimated at about EUR 40/t on Brazilian pig iron). Calebe can do what carbon accountants usually cannot: turn verified emissions into a price premium or contract clause, sell it to the EU buyer in EUR, and run it in EN/PT/ES.

Who buys

Three paying layers. (1) EUR payers: EU importers, traders and foundries buying Brazilian merchant pig iron, semi-finished steel, fasteners and aluminium. They pay a CBAM cost on default values unless their supplier provides verified actual data. Add EU timber and furniture importers who become EUDR operators on 30 Dec 2026, and EU sustainability consultancies or verifiers that need a Portuguese-speaking field partner in Brazil. (2) BRL payers: mid-size SC and Brazilian producers. In SC that means fastener makers in Joinville (CN 7318 is in CBAM scope), steel processors, foundries, pine and furniture exporters, and suppliers answering EU VSME/PCF requests. (3) Digital buyers: ESG analysts and engineers who buy templates.

Brazil side: SINDIFER (MG pig iron producers), Aco Brasil, ABAL (aluminium), ABIMCI (processed wood, Curitiba), ABIMOVEL and Sindimoveis Sao Bento do Sul, FIESC events in Florianopolis/Joinville, Sebrae-SC/ApexBrasil PEIEX consultants, AHK Brasil-Alemanha and Italcam. When they buy

(a) An EU buyer's CBAM declarant asks the Brazilian supplier for installation data, or sees the 2026 default markup in its first cost estimate. Certificate sales start in 2027, so 2027 budgets are being set now (Q4 2026). (b) An EU timber importer has to file a Due Diligence Statement from 30 Dec 2026 and needs plot polygons. (c) An EU customer sends an ESG questionnaire and the SC supplier wants to answer at VSME level only. (d) A new EU contract is negotiated under the Mercosur interim agreement tariff cuts.

How value is extracted

Entry point: the free calculator and US$79-149 templates capture emails and identify buyers by CN code and tonnage. Core: a fixed-fee pack (D1 EUR 4,500, D3 EUR 1,800 or D4 EUR 2,900), always quoted with the EUR/yr saving next to it. Upsell 1: the D2 premium/clause memo at EUR 2,000 bundled, because data with no price move leaves the money on the table. Upsell 2: the R1 retainer at EUR 300-650/mo, sold at handover because CBAM data is annual, EUDR plots change and questionnaires recur. Upsell 3: extra routes or installations at EUR 1,500 each. Two-sided capture: when an EU importer pays for D1, offer the Brazilian supplier D2 in BRL. The supplier now has verified data and can price it. Referral income: introductions to accredited verifiers, whose fees run EUR 5k-50k per installation (SOURCED, cbamguide). Ask for 10% on referred engagements; whether verifiers pay this is UNKNOWN, so treat it as upside only. Cross-sells to other streams: sustainability-premium (green premium pricing method), pro-firm-pricing (EUR consulting rate card), model-templates (T1/T2/T3 also listed there), br-tax-reform (the same SC exporters face CBS/IBS export credit questions), directory (a 'CBAM/EUDR providers in Brazil' list with paid listings for verifiers and GIS firms), b2b-referrals, deal-channel (exporters with verified low-carbon data are better M&A targets), yt-finance (explainer videos).

Unit economics

Buyer value (SOURCED): one estimate puts the default-value cost uplift on Brazilian pig iron at about EUR 40/t at EUR 80/t CO2 (eurometal). The 2026 markup is 10%, rising to 20% in 2027 and 30% from 2028 (OPIS/eurometal). Verifier fees are EUR 5k-50k per installation (cbamguide). Competitor supplier-side tools are often free (CBAMBOO supplier side; importer plans EUR 9k-19k/yr). SAP's CBAM module is EUR 990/mo with a 3-year minimum. CALC, buyer ROI: a 10,000 t/yr pig iron flow x EUR 40/t = EUR 400k/yr exposure. A EUR 4,500 D1 plus EUR 2,000 D2 is about 1.6% of one year's gap even if only half the gap is recovered. That is the price anchor; never quote hours. CALC, delivery margin: D1 at EUR 4,500 and about 20 h for the first one (12 h from the 3rd onwards) gives EUR 225-375/h. D3 at EUR 1,800 and about 8 h gives EUR 225/h. D4 at EUR 2,900 and about 14 h gives EUR 207/h. R1 at EUR 650/mo and about 2.5 h/mo gives EUR 260/h. ASSUMPTION, conversion: calculator leads to call 15%, call to pack 25%, so 1 pack per about 27 qualified leads. Pack to retainer 40%. SKU buyers: 1-3% of SEO visitors to product pages. CALC, net: fees and FX about 3%, Brazilian tax 6-15% under Simples (ASSUMPTION; an export of services to an EU client may be exempt from ISS/PIS/COFINS; confirm with an accountant). Planning net is about 82-88% of gross. FX: EUR to USD at 1.08 (ASSUMPTION).

Kill or scale

Kill or shrink: if by end of month 4 there are fewer than 2 paid packs and calculator leads are under 25, keep only the SKUs on autopilot (about 1 h/week) and move the hours to sustainability-premium or pro-firm-pricing. Also shrink if the EUDR review or CBAM amendments remove Brazilian wood or pig iron demand (watch EUR-Lex). Scale: if 2 or more EUR-paid D1s close and partner referrals arrive without outreach, raise D1 to EUR 6,500. Hire a Brazilian junior environmental engineer (about BRL 5-7k/mo as PJ, ASSUMPTION) to run data intake. Add an aluminium and ferro-alloy page set. Pitch a fixed-fee 'Brazil supplier data programme' to one EU importer or trader covering all of its Brazilian installations (EUR 20-40k).

Risks

1) Regulatory flux. The EUDR review (due 30 Apr 2026; outcome not confirmed by my searches, UNKNOWN), the CBAM downstream extension and the VSME delegated act (expected Q3 2026, final adoption UNKNOWN) can shift scope. Mitigate by selling short fixed-scope packs and free SKU updates. 2) Liability and role confusion. Calebe is not an accredited verifier, lawyer or certified LCA practitioner. Use an engagement letter with a scope disclaimer, a liability cap at the fee, and professional indemnity insurance (cost UNKNOWN). 3) Verifier bottleneck. Accreditations only began around Sep 2026, so verified data may slip, delaying the buyer's saving and the D2 premium. 4) Free competition. Supplier-side software is free (CBAMBOO) and big consultancies (SGS, Bureau Veritas, Brazilian ESG firms) bundle data prep. The defence is price, speed, PT/ES language and the pricing memo. 5) Small 2026 SC CBAM base. Most SC exports (meat, motors, furniture) are not CBAM goods today, so CBAM revenue will come largely from MG/ES pig iron and EU buyers, not SC. EUDR and VSME are the SC entry. 6) The 50 t/yr importer threshold excludes tiny importers. 7) FX and payment: EUR revenue is exposed to BRL swings and Payoneer/Wise fees. 8) The US tariff on Brazil diverts exporters to the EU. This raises demand, but also competition from other consultants.

Brazil notes

Entity: Sociedade Limitada Unipessoal under Simples Nacional, CNAE 7020-4/00 (business consulting), Anexo III if the payroll ratio (fator R) is 28% or more via pro-labore, otherwise Anexo V. ASSUMPTION; confirm with an accountant. EUR/USD clients (EU importers, verifiers, EU consultancies) count as an export of services when the result happens abroad. Under LC 116/2003 art. 2 I the export is generally exempt from ISS and PIS/COFINS; under Simples the export revenue is segregated. Confirm this for each contract, because EUDR fieldwork done in Brazil for a Brazilian plot could be argued to have its 'result' in Brazil. Receive funds through Payoneer or Wise Business EUR/USD receiving details and close the cambio in the provider app; keep invoices in EN. Brazilian exporter clients must generally be billed in BRL (domestic contracts in foreign currency are restricted under Lei 14.286/2021 rules), with an NFS-e from the municipality (ISS about 2-5%). Price these clients at the EUR list converted on the day. Digital SKUs go through Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad as merchant of record (they handle EU VAT); payouts come via bank/PayPal (exact Brazil payout rails UNKNOWN; check in the dashboard). Local leverage: Sebrae-SC/ApexBrasil PEIEX consultants (the programme was renewed at FIESC), FIESC export events, Sindimoveis Sao Bento do Sul, ABIMCI in Curitiba, and SINDIFER (MG pig iron). Under the EU-Mercosur interim agreement (provisionally applied since 1 May 2026), EU buyers are re-sourcing from Brazil, which makes this timely.
