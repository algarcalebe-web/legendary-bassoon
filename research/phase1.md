## The "long interview" claim: not supported by what we found
| Item | Finding | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry forms | Black Tomato ~10-12 fields (from site JS); Journeys by Design 11 inputs | P |
| Discovery call | 15-30 min (Travelweek Jul 2026); 30 min (Aracari); 30-45 min (Forest Travel); ~15 min welcome call (Zicasso) | vendor |
| Time to first proposal | 24-48 h (Aracari); 2 business days (Zicasso); few days to 1-2 weeks (Forest Travel) | vendor |
| Revision rounds | 2-3 (Forest Travel, only source) | vendor |
| Preference capture quality | Zicasso says forms cannot capture "the why". 59% of travellers start with no destination (Expedia/Luth 2023). No independent data on repeated questions or generic plans | weak |

The measured interview is short. The plausible pain is elsewhere: clients don't know what they want, the brief misses "the why", and revisions follow. That is a hypothesis, not a finding.

## Verdict on the core claim
Planning takes real time (5-9 h of content, 71-day consideration window) and a large minority find it stressful (33-47%). Counter-evidence: anticipation and planning are the happiest phase (Nawijn 2010, n=1,530), and the highest stress figures are still minorities. For affluent travellers specifically, nothing was measured: no hours, no stress, no regret data. Revealed willingness to pay exists (Centurion US$15,000 in year one, Platinum US$895 a year, Global Rescue US$375-1,325 a year, Medjet US$399+) but buys access, rescue and service, not planning time. **The claim is partly supported for travellers generally, unproven for affluent travellers, and its "long questionnaire" component is contradicted by vendor-stated process lengths.**

## Gaps and limits
- No first-person affluent traveller quotes (Reddit, FlyerTalk, Trustpilot block this environment). Seven advisor-side quotes in `raw/quotes_v3.md`.
- Unverified against primaries: Expedia (PDF 404), Greetwell (URL 404), Wyndham (release not found), DOT, Eurocontrol, ACI Europe (blocked), SITA per-1,000 rate (gated).
- Process figures are vendor claims. Scott Dunn, A&K, Matuete and Quintessentially process data not found.
- Corridor pains (Brazilians in Italy; North Americans/Europeans in Brazil) mostly unmeasured. Brazil spent US$21.7bn abroad in 2025 (BCB, S).
