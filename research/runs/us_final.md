## 4. Rules and traps

1. IDENTITY: Upwork needs a close-up face photo (logos and AI photos banned) and shows the ID-verified legal name on contracts and invoices; ID checks can come any time with 7 days to comply (https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/17995641806739--How-to-choose-a-good-profile-picture ; https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/9908325862163-ID-Verification). Contra requires ID verification (https://contra.com/blog/Ur627kqN-getting-verified-on-contra). Fiverr says the profile image can represent services but ID verification may be demanded (https://block.fiverr.com/content/rt/new-listing-experience-email). Whether a studio name is accepted anywhere: NONE FOUND. Never invent a team or credentials.
2. FIXED FEES ONLY: no percentage of capital raised, finder fees or investor introductions (US broker-dealer risk; general knowledge, UNVERIFIED). The EIC flags exclusivity, IP and success-fee terms and has a consultant code of conduct (https://eic.ec.europa.eu/news/eic-board-observations-use-consultants-eic-applications-2025-02-28_en). Contingent-fee limits such as FAR 52.203-5 are UNVERIFIED recall.
3. NOT REGULATED ADVICE: no 409A valuations or fairness opinions; disclaimers, limitation of liability, version log; no promises of uplift, funding or award; no CFA/CPA claims; no YC, Techstars, EIC or Commission affiliation language or logos.
4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND IP: do not reuse Advent, Whirlpool, Deneffe or Mercor data, models, templates, benchmarks or task content; NDA work cannot be portfolio; write original material, not Hult slides or paid-course tables; OpinionX asks attribution if its template is reused. Read the Deneffe contract (non-compete, non-solicit, exclusivity, framework IP) and Mercor terms before listing anything pricing-related.
5. CLIENT DATA: keep clients siloed (antitrust information-sharing risk, UNVERIFIED); use only AI tools whose terms bar training on inputs; offer an NDA and delete-on-request; expect GDPR/LGPD and DPA requests.
6. ENTITY: MEI cap is R$81,000 a year (about R$6,750 a month; the R$150,000 proposal PLP 67/2025 is not law) and consulting is generally not an MEI activity (https://simplifique.contmatic.com.br/blogs/mei-2026-limite-faturamento-o-que-muda ; https://www.contabilidadezen.com.br/blog/cnae-exportacao-servicos-2026/). At about R$50-52k a month the cap is gone in about 1.6 months (CALC). Options: CNPJ in Simples (Annex III from 6%, Annex V from 15.5%, Fator R 28%: https://valorfinal.com.br/tabela-fator-r), Lucro Presumido, or personal carnê-leão. Fator R at about R$52k implies a pro-labore of about R$14.6k a month with its own INSS/IRPF (CALC).
7. EXPORT EXEMPTIONS ARE CONDITIONAL: ISS and PIS/COFINS non-incidence need the result verified abroad, foreign-currency inflow under an exchange contract and an export NFS-e; consulting used in Brazil gets none (Cosit SC 117/2015 and 78/2019 as cited by secondary sources: https://tributodevido.com.br/portal/criterios-para-nao-incidencia-de-pis-cofins-na-exportacao-de-servicos-no-simples-nacional/ ; https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/tributacao-pj-trabalha-exterior-guia-2026/).
