## 4. Best single relaxation
**P4 passivity.** It is the only relaxation that turns the volume problem into an hours budget that fits in 40 hours a week. About 129 billed hours a month at US$90 net-to-talent reaches US$10k (CALC: 10,000 / (90 x 0.86), about 30 h/week; about 152 h or 35 h/week if the income is individual-paid at 0.73). The arithmetic is feasible only at US$80+/h net-to-talent and high utilisation, and the corrected base case still misses (about US$9.27k). The downside is that he trades the asset ambition for a second job. Income stops when hours stop, nothing compounds, and he may collide with his ET-hours job and its moonlighting clause. Utilisation, not rate, is the binding constraint.

**Runner-up: P2 at T1 only**, which is permission to apply to posted demand. The hours routes need it, and it costs only application time. P1 comes third. It brings Toptal and the platforms, but the 3-6% odds make it a lottery ticket. Relaxing P3 or P5 alone does not help.
