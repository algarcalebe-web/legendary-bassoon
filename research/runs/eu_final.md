## RULES AND TRAPS

- **Success fees.**
  - Not banned, but the EIC Board flags exclusivity, IP and success-fee terms. Consultants' success fees are reportedly not eligible EC costs. Source: https://eic.ec.europa.eu/news/eic-board-observations-use-consultants-eic-applications-2025-02-28_en
  - Whether success fees can be charged to a grant is UNVERIFIED. Offer flat fixed fees only, no success fee, no exclusivity, and leave deliverable IP with the client.
  - The Code of Conduct is voluntary. It requires fee transparency and success-rate disclosure, and a subcontracting consultancy must disclose subcontracting to its client. Source: https://eic.ec.europa.eu/news/eic-board-publishes-code-conduct-consultants-offering-services-eic-applicants-2023-11-06_en
- **Funding claims.** Never claim a consultant is necessary or imply funding, endorsement, or EIC/EC logos. Never say "fundable from your grant". Proposal-preparation costs are likely ineligible (UNVERIFIED). Subcontracting rules apply to funded projects. Source: https://www.ffg.at/en/europe/heu/legal-financial/theme_subcontracting
- **Conflict of interest.** Do not serve competing applicants in the same call. Do not combine consulting with expert-evaluator status for the same call (ASSUMPTION). Evaluator pay is up to EUR 450/day. Source: https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/about-us/key-documents/experts_es
- **Non-compete and NDA.** Check your contracts with Deneffe Consulting, Mercor, Advent International and Whirlpool for non-compete, confidentiality, moonlighting, IP-assignment and "AI-lab work" clauses. Treat them only as credentials. Do not name clients or reuse client frameworks. Mercor work may restrict competing AI-training offerings (UNVERIFIED). Legal review is advised.
- **Marketplace identity.**
  - Upwork needs a real or everyday-use name, allows one account per person, and shows the last name as an initial. Source: https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/9127142196243-How-to-represent-yourself-authentically-on-Upwork
  - Fiverr allows a business name and logo, with private ID verification. Source: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010558598-Creating-your-seller-profile
  - Both ban taking payment off-platform. Fiverr: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601-Stay-protected-Fiverr-s-off-platform-policy
  - Malt does not list Brazil and requires a verified registered company outside its ten countries. Source: https://help.malt.com/kb/guide/en/i-am-a-freelancer-registered-abroad-for-which-countries-does-malt-authorize-registration-in-this-situation-1qLLe7BZH8/Steps/2511427
- **Merchant-of-record rules.**
  - Lemon Squeezy bans services and consulting. Source: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products
  - Paddle bans pure consulting and ghostwriting. Source: https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle
  - Sell products on a MoR. Take service orders on marketplaces or by direct invoice.
- **EU VAT.**
  - Digital goods: a MoR collects and remits consumer VAT. Gumroad has been MoR since Jan 2025 (secondary: https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees). Lemon Squeezy: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/payments/sales-tax-vat
  - B2B services to EU businesses are normally reverse-charged. The EU customer self-assesses and you invoice without VAT, quoting their VAT ID (secondary: https://lookuptax.com/docs/reverse-charge). Verify with an adviser.
  - Withholding tax on fees to non-residents in some EU countries is UNVERIFIED.
- **Brazil invoicing and tax (all secondary; get a contador).**
  - MEI (about R$81k/year cap) is unusable at this scale. A CNPJ under Simples Nacional is needed. Source: https://exiap.com.br/guias/mei-pode-prestar-servico-para-o-exterior
  - Service exports: ISS is generally not due if the result is verified abroad (LC 116/2003 art. 2, I). Source: https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp116.htm
  - PIS/COFINS are non-incident on exports with foreign-currency inflow. Source: https://tributodevido.com.br/portal/exportacao-de-servicos-no-simples-nacional-3/
  - Issue an export NFS-e and keep the FX contract. COSIT SC 185/2025 says a result in Brazil is not an export even if a foreigner pays. Source: https://rota.apet.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SC-Cosit-no-185-2025.pdf
  - Open question: whether a MoR or marketplace counts as the "foreign customer". Unresolved.
  - Fator R (28%) decides Annex III vs V. Export revenue counts toward Fator R. Source: https://www.contabilidadezen.com.br/blog/fator-r-aplicado-exportador-servicos-2026/
  - IOF on export inflows: Decreto 6.306 art. 15-B gives 0% per Felsberg. Source: https://www.felsberg.com.br/iof-zero-na-exportacao/. A conflicting blog says 0.38%. COSIT 246/2018 says funds held abroad and sent later may be taxed (PGFN disagrees). Source: https://www.ibet.com.br/iof-cambio-exportacao-e-a-solucao-de-consulta-cosit-246-2018/. Worst case at US$10k/month is about US$38/month.
  - Dividend trap: Lei 15.270/2025 art. 6-A applies 10% withholding on distributions above R$50k/month from one company to one resident individual. It sits right at the US$10k threshold (about R$52-55k). A TRF-4 injunction exists. Source: https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/lei/l15270.htm and https://www.klalaw.com.br/lei-15-270-2025-tributacao-dividendos-controversias/
  - An exchange-contract threshold of "USD 10,000 per receipt" is not supported (it concerns cash). Ask your bank.
  - Receiving as an individual (carnê-leão) is taxed up to 27.5%. Source: https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/meu-imposto-de-renda/tabelas/2026
  - A contador costs about R$300-800/month (ASSUMPTION), so strict "zero capital" is already breached.
- **GDPR and LGPD.** Client decks include personal data. An anonymous operator conflicts with controller-identity disclosure. Use an NDA, DPA/SCC terms and short retention. Brazil adequacy is UNVERIFIED.
