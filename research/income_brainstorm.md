## Licence and regulatory traps (check before charging)

- **Real-estate brokerage** needs a CRECI registration; referral agreements with a licensed broker are the usual way around it.
- **Insurance and consórcio broking** needs a SUSEP licence.
- **Investment advice, research and fund distribution** are regulated by the CVM; publishing recommendations needs an analyst registration, and distributing funds needs a registered distributor.
- **Legal and immigration advice** belongs to lawyers (OAB); work with a partner lawyer and sell logistics and paperwork.
- **Commercial representatives** are governed by Law 4.886/65 and may need registration with the regional council.
- **Tax set-up:** the individual micro-entrepreneur (MEI) category covers only certain activities and consulting generally falls outside it; confirm with a Brazilian accountant before invoicing.
- **Success fees** for introducing deals or capital are sensitive under securities rules; use retainers where in doubt.

## Avoid

Multi-level marketing, trading robots, forex and binary-options signals, crypto 'signals', dropshipping-guru courses, and any offer of 'automatic income' that asks you to pay first or recruit others. They fail the compliance column and most fail the evidence test.

- **This week (days to cash):** fixed-fee modelling briefs, admissions and interview coaching, tutoring, ghostwriting, valuation reports and due-diligence checks.
- **Within 30 days, recurring:** a fractional CFO retainer, a Portuguese course pre-sold on Hotmart, templates, the paid community.
- **Commission, no capital, 1–3 months:** university recruitment agent, export agent, sourcing agent, luxury travel advisor, Santa Catarina manufacturer representation, citizenship paperwork.
- **Compounding, 6+ months:** SEO site, channel, newsletter, micro-SaaS.
- **Bigger plays:** buy-side search for a small business; sell-side mandates.
