The answer

No route reaches US$10k a month while staying faceless, inbound-only, zero-capital and passive. Across 55 skeptic checks, nothing passed with all five pillars intact. No source showed anyone earning US$10k a month that way.

What does work is bending one pillar: passivity. If you accept 34–40 active hours a week, an hours stack can reach US$10k. The stack is one anchor client or contract at US$80–85/h plus 2–3 AI-data platforms as filler. Applying to posted jobs and passing private ID or video checks come with it. The likelier result is US$4–8k net by month 12.

Commissions and matchmaking are real but ride on traffic you own. The strongest automatic ideas for you are a travel affiliate layer inside your itineraries, B2B referral bounties (Deel pays US$1,500 per paying customer), and a niche job board connecting employers with LatAm finance talent. See Automatic ideas.

Combine streams. No single automatic stream reaches US$10k, but several running together (mini-courses, model templates, a faceless finance YouTube channel, itineraries in three languages, affiliate links, optional 1-1 sessions) can add US$1.5–5k a month by month 24 on top of the hours leg, and keep paying when the hours stop. See Plan D.

The passive ideas (templates, itineraries, apps, data, SEO) are side assets, not the path to US$10k on their own. Each needs roughly 130–1,000 sales a month and 24–54 months.

Uncomfortable truths

  1. No single relaxed pillar produced a strong fit (4–5 out of 5). The best skeptic-checked option scored 3 out of 5, and skeptics cut most proposer ratings by 1–2 points.
  2. Mercor's finance and private-equity listings at about US$100/h list 13 countries, and Brazil is not one of them. The Brazil-named finance listing pays US$80/h. Other AI-data platforms advertise US$15–65/h for finance (micro1 US$30–65, Outlier US$30–40, Alignerr US$15–60).
  3. Marketplaces are shrinking. In Q2 2026 Upwork's gross services volume fell 4% and Fiverr's active buyers fell 21.9%. AI is cutting the price of commodity models and slides.
  4. Toptal accepts under 3% of applicants. Skeptics put a Toptal-led route at 3–6% by month 12. Apply anyway: it costs nothing.
  5. Faceless costs conversion. Your NDA work can't be shown, so trust has to come from public sample models you build from public or synthetic data.
  6. Your contracts could block any of this. The Deneffe and Mercor agreements, and any employment moonlighting clause, are unread. Pricing offers overlap Deneffe the most. Valuation, modelling and FP&A are safer.
  7. Every income probability in this atlas is a judgment, not data. Earnings base rates were NONE FOUND for almost every route.

Why stacking works

  • One piece of work sells five ways: a DCF lesson becomes a US$29 course, a US$49 model, a slide in a US$149 bundle, a Spanish and Portuguese edition, and a reason to book a review.
  • Streams share the same buyers, so each one markets the others: every template links to its course, every course sells the bundle and the 1-1 review.
  • Diversification: if Etsy ranking drops or a platform changes fees, the other streams keep paying.
  • It runs alongside the hours leg and keeps paying when hours stop.

What stacking does not do

  • Add up to US$10k by itself within 24 months: the automated layer totals US$0.3–8.5k a month in the table (base ≈US$2.7k), under 5–10% odds of US$10k alone.
  • Remove the setup cost: about 10–15 hours to build each course with its template, and ongoing support.
  • Escape platform fees: Udemy keeps 63% of organic sales; Gumroad 10% + US$0.50 direct, 30% via Discover; Hotmart ≈9.9%.
  • Stay faceless if you add 1-1 sessions: those bend the faceless pillar and are hours, not passive.

Where to sell

  • Udemy (English and Spanish): built-in discovery; you keep 37% of organic sales and 97% of sales through your own link.
  • Your own storefront (Gumroad, Payhip or Podia): higher margin, needs your own traffic; Brazil payout for Gumroad is UNVERIFIED.
  • Hotmart or Kiwify for Portuguese editions: largest Brazilian course market, but paid in BRL.
  • LinkedIn text posts and a free email list drive sales of finance templates best, but need a visible name (bends faceless).

Rules and risks

  • All material must be original: no Harvard cases, Hult or university slides, or CFA Institute content, and no CFA mark in titles.
  • Personal-finance and portfolio courses teach methods (TVM, Sharpe, efficient frontier, risk profile); never recommend specific securities (CVM rules).
  • No Deneffe client material; pricing courses use public examples. Ask Deneffe in writing first.
  • Course earnings data for this niche: NONE FOUND in the research. Gumroad's median creator made ≈US$70 in 2020; full-time Hotmart producers average ≈R$11,959 a month (FGV). Test with 2–3 courses first.
  • Languages: English, Portuguese and Spanish, translated with AI and checked by you; trip plans the same.

Automatic and commission ideas

An extensive sweep of ideas that pay without you trading hours once they run: commissions, referrals, matchmaking between buyers and sellers, royalties, and automated channels. Commission income is real, but it is paid on traffic or relationships you own. The automation starts after the audience exists. Six that fit you best

  1. Travel affiliate layer inside your itineraries and channel. GetYourGuide and Viator ≈8% per booked tour, SafetyWing 10% recurring on travel insurance, Booking.com shares part of its commission. Every itinerary you already wrote has 10–30 bookable links.
  2. B2B referral bounties inside finance content. Deel pays US$500 per sales-qualified referral plus US$1,000 per paying customer (official affiliate page). 7 paying customers a month ≈ US$10.5k.
  3. Niche job board: connect employers with talent. Example: remote finance, pricing and FP&A jobs open to LatAm. Employers pay ≈US$99–299 per post. Japan Dev reported ≈US$60k a month; RanchWork ≈US$7.4k (self-reported).
  4. Paid directory or marketplace. Vetted startup consultants, grant writers, or Santa Catarina suppliers for foreign buyers. Listing and featured fees; commission on matches later.
  5. Talent and client referral bonuses. Refer clients or talent to Toptal, Braintrust and similar networks that pay referral bonuses (amounts UNVERIFIED).
  6. Brazil deal channels (BRL). Telegram and WhatsApp promotion channels with Amazon, Shopee and Mercado Livre affiliate links posted by bots. Shopee pays ≈5–14% by category (vendor blog). Paid in BRL.

"Sistema Dólar Automático" and similar courses

  • What they usually teach: affiliate marketing for foreign offers (ClickBank, Digistore24, BuyGoods), paid in dollars, promoted with Google or YouTube ads to US and European buyers.
  • The model is real. Commissions on those platforms are often 50–75% per sale, so one sale can pay US$50–150.
  • "Automatic" only after you find a campaign where ad cost per sale is below the commission. Until then you pay for ads: plan US$300–1,500 of tests (ASSUMPTION), which bends zero capital.
  • Risks: Google suspends affiliate ad accounts for policy breaches; many offers are supplements and health products with refunds and weak quality; refunds claw back commissions.
  • No Reclame Aqui page for that exact product name came up; related courses ("Vendedor Automático", "Protocolo Venda em Dólar") have complaints about hidden costs and broken guarantees.
  • Verdict: a real but ad-funded gamble, fit 2/5. Better with your own finance or travel offers than with supplements.

The one that fits you: a faceless finance-education channel

  • Finance and investing pay among the highest YouTube rates: ≈US$12–25 per 1,000 views (secondary, modelled).
  • Voice-over, screen and slides only: your models, valuations and pricing teardowns, no face needed.
  • It is the free traffic your course library lacks: every video links to a course, a template and a bundle.
  • Ads alone: US$10k needs ≈500–800k views a month (CALC). With course sales on top, far fewer views are needed.
  • Only ≈2.6% of channels are in the YouTube Partner Program; plan 12–24 months to monetise.

How to tell a real path from a scam

  • Real: shows costs, failure rates, months to profit and ad spend. Scam: "US$10k in 30 days", screenshots of revenue not profit.
  • Real: you sell to customers. Scam: the main buyers are other students (MLM-style course chains).
  • Real: the model works without the guru's paid community. Scam: endless upsells to "inner circle" tiers.
  • Avoid entirely: forex or crypto signals, copy-trading, matched betting sold as income, MLM, "passive" bots.
