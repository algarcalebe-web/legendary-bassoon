# Stress, worry, regret, decision fatigue: raw evidence

Method note: all figures come from search-result snippets. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network proxy (travelweekly.co.uk, meininger-hotels.com, personal.eur.nl), so sample sizes and methods are often missing. Anything marked "n/s" was not stated in what I could see. No figure was rounded or invented. Strength: strong = large sample with stated method, regulator or peer-reviewed; weak = vendor/PR survey, small or undated.

## Not found

- Any survey that segments planning stress, decision fatigue or regret by wealth or income (HNW, affluent). Virtuoso and Amex results found are about demand and amenities, not stress. Whether affluent travellers differ is unproven.
- Hard prevalence figures for tourism regret. The Journal of Travel Research 2025 study is qualitative (49 interviews).
- Travel-specific evidence of forgetting recommendations, itineraries, restaurant picks or stated preferences. Only generic bookmark and revisit evidence was found, from non-travel samples.
- Any regulator or large peer-reviewed survey on "needing a holiday from the holiday". Closest: Wyndham 2017 (30% not relaxed until day 2 or later) and Nawijn 2010.
- Scam-anxiety figures for leisure travellers (not searched in depth).
- Sample sizes and methods for the KAYAK, Skyscanner, Carnival, Kuoni and MEININGER surveys. Pages were blocked or only snippets were available.
- Full-text verification of the Park & Jang n, the Nawijn abstract details and the Aalborg/Hanken paper journal name.
