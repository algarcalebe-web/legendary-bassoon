## Not found

- Hours spent planning, split by affluent or HNW travellers, from Amex, Virtuoso, YouGov, Skift, Phocuswright or similar. The only affluent data found was behavioural (share who research online), with no time figures.
- Number of revision rounds, number of emails or messages, or the length of preference questionnaires for any named operator (Black Tomato, Scott Dunn, Abercrombie & Kent, Matueté, Fora or Virtuoso advisors).
- Discovery-call duration for the named operators. The only duration seen was a 1-hour call at a small seller.
- Days or weeks to first proposal from the named operators. The only figure was "up to 9 business days" at one advisory, with unconfirmed attribution.
- Traveller first-person accounts that quantify hours spent.
- Peer-reviewed quantification of time lost to planning. The Expedia study is a vendor study.
- The Expedia sample size, the full method and the fieldwork dates, because the PDF was blocked. Still needed: that PDF, the Black Tomato page and the Travolution page, fetched from an unblocked network.
- Google or Think with Google US touchpoint data from the last 3 years. Only India and older material surfaced.
