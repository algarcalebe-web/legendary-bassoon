Strength key: High = primary page read or regulator; Med = named publisher/vendor price page seen via snippet, or two consistent sources; Low = single snippet, undated, SEO/market-research firm, or vendor claim.

## Summary reading: best-evidenced WTP pools (for caller)

1. Altrata (primary, via secondary): UHNW luxury spend USD 290bn (2024); jets+yachts 28.6bn; hospitality 25.3bn (earlier edition says 45bn travel/hospitality; conflicting).
2. Ultra-premium airport: PS $4,850/yr All Access, $3,550 member suite, $4,850 non-member suite; Windsor Heathrow from GBP 3,812 per visit (page read). Best evidence: two to three consistent sources.
3. Island/villa buyouts: $35k to $199k+/night (Maldives), Necker $77.5k+ per night. Vendor rate cards, Low-Med.
4. Superyacht charter: EUR 120-200k/wk at 40 m up to 700k/wk; APA ~35%; broker 15-20%; market ~$3.2bn (single mill, Low). Running costs 10-20% of value per year.
5. Jet charter: ~USD 16bn market (two mills agree); broker markup 10-20%; empty legs ~14% of flights at 25-75% off.
6. Concierge health: PinnacleCare $15-55k/yr, retainers from $100k; Fountain Life $10.5k/$21.5k/$85k. Vendor pages (Med).
7. Security: UK GBP 500-1,000/day; US $1,800-4,000/day high-end, $36-75k/month single-agent retainer. Compare: K&R insurance ~$1-2k/yr and Global Guardian $285-485/yr: people pay far more for guards than for insurance/subscriptions.
8. Access events: Wimbledon Centre Court debenture GBP 116k (5 yr), Art Basel VIP $5-15k, Monaco hospitality EUR 6.8k+, reservation resale $350-1,700 (Appointment Trader takes 20-30%).
9. Privacy: consumer data removal $39-$400/yr; FAA LADD/PIA free; no UHNW-tier price found.
10. Gaps: FBO fees, yacht management % fee, Crisis24/ISOS individual prices, Private Health Management, aircraft privacy trust costs, OSINT retainers.
