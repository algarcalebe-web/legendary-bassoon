### (1) Top pains with evidence
1. Rules keep changing under people already moved/applying: Portugal real-estate route closed, Spain closed, Greece threshold doubled, Italy flat tax tripled in 18 months, Portugal citizenship timeline lengthened (2026). Evidence above. Pain = "programme risk"; grandfathering is partial.
2. Residency is a multi-year, multi-advisor project with 42,390 Greek files pending (Nov 2025) and no single tracker of where a family is in each process.
3. Empty-house risk: leaks, freeze, theft; insurance denies/limits after 30-60 unoccupied days; case with GBP 566k paid while owners abroad.
4. Staffing multi-homes: estate manager US$150-250k plus NDA premium; per-property overhead; key-person risk; no shared pool across properties.
5. Average 3 residences per UHNW but utilisation per home not found: low-use homes are the fixed-cost problem (carrying ~1% maintenance, ~5% total operating on mass-market rule of thumb).
6. Moving is also a tax-residency problem (see D7): leaving UK means 3-10 year IHT tail.

### (1) Top pains with evidence
1. Day-count audit risk: any partial day counts (NY); FTB triangulates card, phone, flight, toll data, so the family's own digital exhaust is the auditor's evidence. Pain = proving presence/absence retrospectively across 3-6 years.
2. Multi-regime rules (NY 183, CA 275 per sources, UK SRT ties, Schengen 90/180, US substantial presence) conflict, no single view per family member.
3. Payment friction on trips: unexpected fees/exchange rates 34%, cannot pay in local currency 24%, preferred card refused 23%, security concern 71-72% (Flywire, US vendor surveys).
4. Family-office fragmentation: slow cross-border payments, poor cash visibility (vendor claims).
5. Valuables in transit/second homes outside standard policy scope.

### (2) Spend pools and prices
- Day-count apps: TaxDay US$9.99/month; Monaeo price not disclosed; so software spend is tiny versus exposure (NY ~US$1bn from 15k audits = ~US$67k average per audit, own arithmetic from secondary figures).
- Family office running cost 36-105 bps of AUM; concierge/lifestyle 20-40% of opex for US$500m+ families (low-strength).
- UHNW luxury goods spend US$290bn/yr (Wealth-X).
- Art/transit premiums, FX spreads, BNPL: not found.

### (4) 2023-2026 shifts
- Authorities' data matching (FTB cards/phones/flight data) pushes proof burden to taxpayers; more movers (record 142k-165k) means more first-time residency-change cases.
- UK non-dom abolition and IHT residence tail create new day-count and ties problems for leavers.
- Travel spend rising: 79-80% plan more spend (vendor surveys).
- Tech: passive location, wallet payments (27% prefer wallets), stablecoin/real-time cross-border rails (no UHNW adoption data found).
