# alt_a: What affluent/HNW leisure travellers use today, and where each option fails (Phase 2)

Method note: 2026-10-06. About 35 tool calls. Almost everything is from WebSearch summaries; a few pages were read via curl (destination.com/Skift Fora pieces, altexsoft Expedia piece, Wikipedia, pulse2). Primary hosts (travelpulse, luxurytraveladvisor, SEC, Skift full text, Trustpilot) were blocked, paywalled or only seen as snippets. Reused from sources.md / advisor_process.md and NOT repeated: Expedia 303 min, Greetwell AI survey, SEO Travel ChatGPT test, Skift 2017 HNW agent-use survey, Virtuoso/YouGov 2019 spend, Black Tomato no-fee, Journeys by Design form/budget floor/deposit, Zicasso free to traveller, Fora "$250+" planning fee, Trustpilot ratings for Black Tomato/A&K, Preferred Hotels/Harris 68% pet-peeve. Strength: strong = primary/regulator/peer-reviewed with method; medium = named source, vendor or secondary, method partly stated; weak = snippet-only, undated or unverified. "Not found" = looked, could not establish. Search-engine summaries produced several wrong results (A&K searches returned Abercrombie & Fitch); those were discarded.

## Cross-cutting takeaways
1. DIY tools are heavily used for ideas but not trusted for transactions (Expedia: 8% comfortable booking via AI; IMG: 8% say AI alone suffices). Nothing found shows HNW satisfaction with DIY.
2. Advisor networks are large and growing (Virtuoso US +21% 2025; Fora 15,000 advisors, 97% novices) but advisor use by $250k+ households was only 17% of past vacations (Harris, 2025) versus 53% intent: the stated gap is the best lead for Phase 2.
3. Operator/DMC evidence on failures is thin; complaints exist but only as single anecdotes. No churn or attrition data for any advisor network.
