# ADVISOR / OPERATOR PROCESS: how bespoke-travel sellers capture preferences, and how long it takes (raw findings)

Method note (read first)
- Research date: 2026-10-06. The brief said the network was fully open. It was not. WebFetch returned EGRESS_BLOCKED for blacktomato.com, scottdunn.com, zicasso.com, kensingtontours.com and abercrombiekent.com. `curl` through the proxy did work for most of those hosts, so primary pages were read with curl plus HTML-to-text where the page was server-rendered.
- Where a page was a JavaScript app (Kensington, A&K, Scott Dunn enquiry, Matuete, Luxury Holiday Company), no content could be read. Those are listed under Not found. Nothing was filled in from memory.
- Rows marked "WebSearch summary only" come from search-result summaries, not from a page I opened. Treat them as leads. One such summary was WRONG when checked: a search summary said Journeys by Design charges a "booking and design fee" of 30%/35%. The T&C page says this is a booking DEPOSIT. The corrected row is below.
- Operator pages are self-reported marketing copy. Zicasso's own blog posts, including its "vs Scott Dunn / A&K / Kensington / Black Tomato" pages, are competitor-marketing and are not used as evidence about those competitors.
- Nothing is invented. "Not found" means I looked and could not read it.

## C. Not found (looked, could not establish)

Operator-specific
- Scott Dunn: enquiry-form field count, call length, days to first proposal, revision rounds, planning fee, minimum spend. The How We Work and Enquire pages are JavaScript-rendered; only a search summary of the generic process was available (call or online form, one named specialist, refine on feedback). No numbers.
- Abercrombie & Kent: enquiry-form fields, call length, days to proposal, rounds, design fee or retainer, minimum spend. Site not readable (JS) and the T&C pages returned empty text. Only a summary-level statement ("Journey Designers"; call travel consultants on 1-800-935-2620) was seen.
- Kensington Tours: all of the above. One search summary says a quote-form submission is followed by contact "in the next 2 business days". I could not open the page, so it is unverified and not tabulated as a fact. No fee, minimum or revision count found.
- Matuete: site (matuete.com) loads but only navigation text could be extracted. No process, fee, or minimum found. A search for the company returned nothing relevant.
- The Luxury Holiday Company (theluxuryholidaycompany.com): the site says "100% tailor-made" and offers a "tailor-maker" inspiration board; no fee, retainer, call length, rounds or days-to-proposal found. Do not use the "£250 per person retainer" and "one working day" text that appeared in one search summary: it came from a result list that mixed in other companies (e.g. Plan South America) and I could not tie it to this operator.
- Exoticca-type sellers: Exoticca sells pre-planned itineraries with customisation, not bespoke design from scratch (search summary). No planning fee or design time found.
- Europe Express: it is a trade-facing FIT/group supplier; its FAQ was read (39KB) but not mined for bespoke-design process figures. Treat as not found.
- Quintessentially: membership prices only from a search summary of old press (e.g. levels at $1,500 / $3,500 / $36,000 and UK GBP 750 / 2,500 / 24,000). Dates and sources not verified, so not tabulated. No trip-design process, call length or proposal timing found.
- Virtuoso (as a network): no advisor intake standard, call length, form length, or rounds. Individual agencies set their own.
- Fora: no discovery-call length, intake-questionnaire length, or fee amounts beyond "$250 or more". Fora's main site returned HTTP 429.
- Journeys by Design: days to first proposal, number of revision rounds, discovery-call length: not stated on pages read. Home-visit length not found.
- Black Tomato: call length, days to first proposal, rounds: not stated. Search results elsewhere refer to a "Panic Button" 24-hour rapid itinerary (seen only as a Zicasso competitor-marketing line, not verified on Black Tomato's own site).
- "Process takes weeks": no operator in the target list was found stating in so many words that the process takes weeks. Closest: Forest Travel (1-2 weeks for a complex first itinerary; start 3-6 months ahead) and Zicasso (start requests 4-8 months ahead). Black Tomato says trips are "refined until it's perfect" with no duration.
