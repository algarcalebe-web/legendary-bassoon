### D2.2 Top pains with evidence
1. Unproven, high-priced, unregulated "longevity" product with no quality referee: CLP CHF 40k/week, HLI to USD 19k, Fountain Life to USD 85k vs ACR no-screen stance, ~30% incidentals, no RCT (see table). Buyers cannot tell evidence-based from theatre. Strength Med.
2. Results do not travel: one-week retreat/diagnostic produces data (MRI, genome, biomarkers) with no owner at home; 1-2 assessments a year (HLI) leaves 50 weeks unmanaged. Inference from structure of offerings; no direct survey found.
3. Continuity of care away from home: records fragmented, prescriptions not recognised, own-copy DIY advice. Evidence Low but consistent; for UHNW with multiple homes the pain is stronger (not measured; no UHNW study found).
4. Overdiagnosis anxiety and cascade (incidentalomas) with no single accountable physician: Med.
5. Parents/older relatives on multigenerational trips: accessibility and medical readiness handled by checklists (Low).
6. Privacy of diagnosis for public figures: cited as reason for Swiss/Gulf VIP checkups (Low, promotional).

### D10.2 Top pains with evidence
1. Reputational/scrutiny risk of private flying: ICCT 19.5 Mt, T&E 5-14x, Greenpeace Davos counts; owners want to fly without being the headline. Med.
2. Offsets are discredited, SAF is scarce and 2-5x price: no trusted, auditable way to "do the right thing" (Carbon Brief, Berkeley quote, SAF price). Med.
3. Impact claims are unverifiable: conservation levies exist (Singita USD 18/night) but guests cannot see outcomes; A&K 70 projects self-reported. Low-Med.
4. Next-gen want experiences/impact (Knight Frank ~half; UBS ~half impact) but travel supply is brochure-led; say/do gap unmeasured. Low-Med.
5. Philanthropic travel is intermediated and generic: big conservation outcomes (rhino translocation) funded by foundations, not by travelling guests; no product turns UHNW travel presence into participation. Inference, Low.
