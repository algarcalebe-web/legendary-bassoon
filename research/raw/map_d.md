### 5.2 Top pains (D5)
1. Crew: 37-48% junior turnover, 72% leave <18 months; owner experience = constant retraining, guest-service inconsistency. Entry wages flat since 2005 vs senior wage inflation.
2. Berth scarcity and peak pricing: 8 leasable >60m berths on the Riviera; EUR 2-2.6k/day for 55m in Capri/Cervo/Portofino.
3. Cost of ownership 10-15% of value per year for a depreciating, low-use asset (utilisation is the core logic of the fractional boom).
4. Refit/yard slot planning (evidence thin: "70% planned too late"; no 2023-26 delay statistic found).
5. Regulatory/reputational heat on private jets (ETS scope widening, Dutch/French pressure, SAF ~2x cost) with no easy consumer-grade offset product aimed at principals.
6. Last-mile fragmentation: heli/eVTOL/ground not integrated; eVTOL timelines repeatedly slipped (Joby Dubai late 2025 to 2026; Archer UAE "at risk"; Lilium failure).

### 5.3 Spend pools and prices
Yacht running cost USD 0.7m (30m) to 4.5m+ (80m); berths USD 78k/month (Antibes 200ft); fractional yacht quarter ~USD 325k/yr plus USD 2.5m entry; NetJets/Flexjet cards ~USD 200-215k per 25h (light jet); fractional jet shares USD 0.5-0.85m entry (broker est.); heli seat EUR 150-245; rail USD 4-10k pp (VSOE) up to USD 102k/journey suite; expedition USD 36k pp (Ponant); Aman at Sea USD 38.5-54.6k; Four Seasons Yachts from ~USD 20k/week.

### 5.4 Incumbents and failure points
Yacht management (Burgess, Camper & Nicholsons, Fraser, N&J, Y.CO, Hill Robinson): fee structures opaque (percent of budget vs retainer; percent figures not found); crew sourced via agencies (Quay, Faststream) rather than retained talent pools. Fractional yacht (IYC, SeaNet, Nexgen, Ahoy Club): small, vendor-claimed demand, unclear governance/resale. Jets: NetJets, Flexjet, VistaJet. Heli: Blade (now Joby), Heli Air Monaco. eVTOL: Joby, Archer, Lilium (failed). Rail/cruise: Belmond/VSOE, Ponant, hotel-brand yachts (Aman, Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton; Ritz-Carlton yacht financials reported weak).

### 5.5 2023-26 shifts
Utilisation mindset toward fractional (jet and yacht); Flexjet capital injection and share gains; Joby consolidating the heli/air-taxi booking channel (Blade, Aug 2025); EU ETS reform proposal Jul 2026; hotel brands entering yachting (2026-27 deliveries); expedition cruising +22%; Schiphol ban retreat shows regulation is slow but local airport bans (Eindhoven) proceed.

### 6.2 Top pains (D6)
1. Scarcity plus gatekeeping: Paddock Club sold out via official channel (2025 Monaco) forcing buyers to resellers at EUR 5.5k+; Met Gala by invitation only; LA28 deposits are cheap but allocation unknown.
2. Official monopolies: LA28 On Location exclusive; F1 Experiences exclusive; buyer has no price comparison.
3. Fraud on secondary/grey market: GBP 9.3m lost in 13 months in UK; verification of authentic hospitality is nobody's job; assistants buy under time pressure.
4. Price inflation of access: Wimbledon debenture +45% to GBP 116k; Cannes hotel EUR 1.7-7.5k/night; Super Bowl suites USD 0.6-2.5m.
5. Logistics bundling of events (hotel, yacht, jet, security) done ad hoc by PAs.
6. Cultural access (after-hours museums, private collections): no price data found; delivered by relationships (Sotheby's Preferred is the only structured offer seen).

### 6.3 Spend pools
Per-event: Paddock Club USD 6-40k pp; Wimbledon debenture GBP 116k (5 yrs); Met Gala USD 75k seat; Super Bowl suite USD 0.6-2.5m; LA28 USD 1.75-15k+ pp; Cannes yacht EUR 60k-1m/week. Art: USD 59.6bn global market with top-end concentration. Auction houses: private sales ~USD 1.1-1.3bn/yr (Sotheby's).

### 6.4 Incumbents and failure points

Resellers: Senate Grand Prix, Grand Prix Events, F1 Destinations, Pearl/others (debenture resale). Failure: no independent authenticity layer, opaque resale pricing, official channel sells out early, allocation favours existing accounts.
