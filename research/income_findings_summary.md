Scope: Calebe Garcia, Santa Catarina. Target: >US$10k/month ADDITIONAL (Deneffe pricing and Mercor are credentials, not income), USD/EUR/global, zero capital, faceless, inbound only, as passive as possible, 40 h/week available.
Basis: three research workflows (broad faceless routes, EU grant-funded startups, US early-stage startups) + 3 spot searches on 2026-10-11. The workflows' final synthesis never completed (weekly limit); this is a manual merge of their journals. Tags: SECONDARY = from search summaries/third-party posts, not primary pages (WebFetch was blocked). ASSUMPTION = my arithmetic input.

## 1. Bottom line
- 0 of ~20 options tested passed all five constraints. No verified case of a solo, faceless, no-outreach seller reaching US$10k/month in any category (income evidence: NONE FOUND).
- US$10k/month is reachable only by relaxing one constraint: hours (70–120 billable h/month), time (18–48 months of catalog compounding), or face/outreach.
- Platform tailwinds are negative: Upwork Q2-26 GSV US$966M (-4%), active clients -4%, workforce -24% (May 2026); Fiverr Q2-26 revenue -10%, active buyers -22% (SECONDARY).

## 3. Fee arithmetic (SECONDARY, verify)
Etsy US$0.20 + 6.5% + 3% + US$0.25 (≈88% net at US$19, ≈90% at US$49; offsite ads 12–15% extra). Gumroad 10% + US$0.50 direct, 30% via Discover. Kiwify ~8.99% + R$2.49; Hotmart ~9.9%. Udemy 37% organic / 97% own link. USD/BRL ≈ 5.22, so US$10k ≈ R$52k.

## 4. New direction: travel plans + finance/strategy template catalog
- Itineraries: no earnings data exist for itinerary sales (Etsy: category not broken out; an Inkfluence guide says only 24 of 2,291 tracked Gumroad products show sales counts and anyone quoting typical income is guessing). ## 6. Recommendation
1. Test, don't commit: ship 15 SKUs (5 itinerary/route, 8 templates, 2 bundles) in 3–4 weeks; measure sales/SKU over 60–90 days. Kill criterion: <0.5 sales/SKU/month at day 90.
2. Price ladder: US$9–19 itinerary; US$29–79 template; US$149–299 bundle; US$490–1,500 async custom edition (form intake, no calls).
3. Faceless storefront (brand name, no face). LinkedIn is the highest-converting free channel for finance/strategy templates but requires a visible voice; treat as an optional test, not a base assumption.
4. To reach US$10k before month 18, add an hours leg (20–25 h/week at US$75–100/h via posted jobs) — it conflicts with "no outreach" if applying counts as outreach.
